FAQ / 常见问题

Q: Why is export unavailable?

A: Ensure your project has at least two frames and verify write permission in the save dialog.

Q: Why can’t I see the window after closing it?

A: Use Dock icon click or menu Window -> Show Main Window to reopen it.

Issue Report Template / 问题反馈模板

When reporting issues, include:

  • App version and macOS version
  • Exact action steps to reproduce
  • Error message screenshot
  • Relevant log snippet (if available)
